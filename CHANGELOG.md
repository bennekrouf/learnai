# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- First release. Ten étapes of AI systems engineering, in French, from how an
  LLM works to designing agents in production: 467 questions, each with a
  reason to unfold, and a lesson for every rubrique.
- The QCM tab comes first: one question at a time, the right answer shown
  once you choose, and a link to the part of the lesson that explains it.
- The Cours tab: short sentences, a **Pourquoi ?** after each claim, and
  diagrams that draw without an internet connection.
- Zoom the page with − and + beside the tabs, or ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0; LearnAI
  remembers the size you read at.
- Drag the border of the course panel on the left to make it wider or
  narrower; a double-click puts it back. The width is kept.
- Your answers are kept between sessions, and the course picks up where you
  left off.
